Careers

Interested in computer repair and helping people solve everyday technology problems? These are example roles that may be relevant to a growing local computer repair business. They are not confirmed current vacancies.

What We Look For

  • Care about the work. We want people who take pride in doing computer repair properly, not just quickly.
  • Straight talk with customers. You will be in someone's home or business explaining what you found. Plain language beats jargon.
  • Reliability. Showing up when you said you would matters more here than any single line on a resume.
  • Respect for the property. Protecting the space, cleaning up, and leaving it better than you found it.
  • Safety, every time. Following safe practice is part of the job, not an extra.

Experience in computer repair or a related trade helps. If you have the attitude and not yet the trade experience, say so, because we do train.

Roles We Hire For

We are a small team, so openings come and go with the work. These are the roles we hire into.

Computer Repair Technician

Diagnose and repair common desktop and laptop hardware and software problems. Work may include replacing components, troubleshooting startup failures, and checking completed repairs.

Qualifications

  • Practical knowledge of desktop and laptop hardware
  • Experience troubleshooting Windows computers
  • Ability to explain technical problems clearly
  • Careful handling of customer equipment and information

Benefits

  • Opportunity to develop practical repair skills
  • Variety of computer troubleshooting work
  • Potential for ongoing technical training
  • Compensation and scheduling discussed for any actual opening

Laptop Hardware Repair Specialist

Focus on laptop screen replacement, keyboard repairs, hinges, storage components, and other model-specific hardware work.

Qualifications

  • Experience disassembling and reassembling laptops
  • Understanding of component compatibility
  • Comfort working with delicate connectors and assemblies
  • Attention to detail and safe repair practices

Benefits

  • Hands-on work with different laptop models
  • Opportunity to build specialized hardware skills
  • Collaborative troubleshooting environment
  • Employment terms discussed if a position becomes available

Customer Support Coordinator

Help customers explain computer problems, organize repair information, communicate estimates, and understand service updates.

Qualifications

  • Clear written and verbal communication
  • Basic familiarity with common computer problems
  • Strong organization and follow-through
  • Respect for customer privacy and confidential information

Benefits

  • Customer-facing technology experience
  • Opportunity to learn computer repair terminology
  • Varied administrative and communication responsibilities
  • Scheduling and compensation determined for any actual opening

Junior Computer Technician

Support routine computer checks, hardware preparation, software installation, and basic troubleshooting under experienced supervision.

Qualifications

  • Interest in computer hardware and software
  • Basic understanding of Windows systems
  • Willingness to learn safe troubleshooting methods
  • Careful documentation and attention to detail

Benefits

  • Practical learning opportunities
  • Exposure to common laptop and desktop repairs
  • Potential for skill development
  • Role-specific terms provided if recruitment opens

How to Apply

Call (703) 997-2971 and ask about hiring. If we are on a job site, leave your name, the role you are interested in, and your trade background, and we will call you back.

We read every application. If there is a fit, expect a short phone conversation first, then a meeting and a ride-along on a job so you can see the work before you commit to it.

We do not always have an opening posted. Send your information anyway. We keep applications on file and reach out first to people who already introduced themselves when a spot opens.
