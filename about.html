About Ashburn Computer Repair

Ashburn Computer Repair is focused on helping Northern Virginia residents make sensible decisions about their computers. We believe good repair service begins with listening, careful troubleshooting, and clear explanations.

Our Story

The illustrative founding story behind Ashburn Computer Repair begins with Daniel Mercer, a computer enthusiast who spent years helping friends and neighbors troubleshoot laptops and desktops. While assisting people around Ashburn, he noticed that many computer owners struggled to find simple explanations of what had gone wrong and whether a repair was worthwhile.

Daniel's approach was shaped by everyday problems: a student's laptop screen breaking during the school year, a home-office computer becoming too slow for work, and a family's concern about photos stored on a failing drive. These situations reinforced the importance of treating each computer as something connected to a person's daily life, rather than just another piece of equipment.

The business story centers on Ashburn and the surrounding Northern Virginia communities. With support from technician Priya Shah and customer coordinator Michael Tran, the proposed team combines hardware troubleshooting, software knowledge, and clear customer communication. The goal is to offer practical repair options while respecting customers' time, data, and budgets.
