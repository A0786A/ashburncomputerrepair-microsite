Keyboard and Hinge Problems We Handle

Laptop keyboards and hinges vary widely between manufacturers. Some keyboards can be replaced separately, while others require more extensive disassembly. Hinges may also damage the surrounding case or display assembly.

Unresponsive Keyboard Keys

Keys that stop responding may indicate keyboard wear, contamination, connection problems, or software issues. We investigate the likely cause.

Broken or Missing Keys

Individual key damage may be repairable on some models. Other laptops require replacement of the keyboard assembly.

Loose Laptop Hinges

Loose hinges can make opening and closing a laptop difficult and may place pressure on the display housing.

Broken Hinge Mounts

Hinge mounting points can separate from the laptop case. We assess whether the damaged components can be repaired or replaced.

Liquid-Damaged Keyboards

Spills may affect the keyboard and nearby internal components. We assess the extent of damage before discussing repair options.
