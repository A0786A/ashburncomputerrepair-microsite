A Local Approach to Computer Repair

Ashburn Computer Repair is built around a straightforward idea: computer owners deserve clear answers, careful troubleshooting, and repairs that make financial sense. Our approach is to listen to the problem, investigate likely causes, and explain the available options before recommending work.

Northern Virginia households rely on computers for everything from remote work and online learning to banking, family communication, and running local businesses. We understand how disruptive a broken laptop or desktop can be and why customers want practical answers without unnecessary technical language.

We believe a computer should not be replaced simply because one component fails. A new screen, keyboard, storage drive, or memory upgrade can sometimes extend a device's useful life. When the cost of repair approaches the value of the computer, we explain that too.

Our goal is to help customers make informed decisions about their technology, protect important information where possible, and get back to the tasks that matter.

What People Ask Us

How much does computer repair cost in Ashburn?

Costs depend on the problem, computer model, and parts required. A software issue may cost less than a major hardware replacement. We discuss estimated pricing after assessing the repair requirements.

Can an older laptop be repaired?

Many older laptops can be repaired or upgraded. Whether the work is worthwhile depends on the device's age, condition, and the cost of parts.

Can you recover files from a broken computer?

Some files can be recovered even when a computer will not start. The likelihood of recovery depends on the condition of the storage device and the cause of the failure.

Do you repair desktop computers and laptops?

We handle a range of desktop and laptop problems, including hardware failures and software issues. Repair availability depends on the specific model and fault.
