Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions people ask Ashburn Computer Repair most about computer repair in Northern Virginia.

How much does computer repair cost in Ashburn, VA?

Computer repair pricing depends on the device, problem, and replacement parts required. Software troubleshooting, screen replacement, and hardware repairs involve different costs. We provide an estimate based on the work needed.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace my laptop?

That depends on the laptop's age, condition, specifications, and repair cost. A screen replacement or SSD upgrade may be worthwhile on an otherwise useful laptop. Major motherboard damage can make replacement more economical.

Can you fix a laptop that will not turn on?

A laptop that will not start may have a battery, power adapter, charging port, storage, or internal hardware problem. We evaluate the symptoms to identify likely causes. Some failures can be repaired, while others require replacement components.

Can files be recovered from a broken hard drive?

Some files may be recoverable depending on the type and severity of damage. Logical errors and physical failures require different approaches. Recovery cannot be guaranteed, and severely damaged drives may need specialist laboratory services.

What should I do if my laptop screen is cracked?

Avoid pressing on the damaged screen or continuing to open and close a badly damaged display assembly. Record the laptop model and describe the visible damage. A compatible replacement panel may restore normal use.

Can an SSD make my old computer faster?

Replacing a traditional hard drive with an SSD can significantly improve startup and application loading times. Results depend on the computer's other components and overall condition. We check compatibility before recommending an upgrade.

How do I know whether my computer has malware?

Possible warning signs include unexpected pop-ups, browser redirects, suspicious programs, and unusual system activity. These symptoms do not always mean malware is present. An assessment can help determine the cause and appropriate response.

What areas of Northern Virginia do you serve?

Our listed service area includes Ashburn, Sterling, Broadlands, Leesburg, Dulles, Loudoun County, and Lansdowne. Service arrangements depend on the specific location and repair requirements. Contact us with your town and computer issue to discuss availability.
